About KaamSetu

KaamSetu is designed to bridge the gap between hardworking individuals seeking employment and recruiters searching for reliable workers.

Many workers such as drivers, security guards, delivery executives, house maids, caretakers, and helpers struggle to access trustworthy digital job platforms. KaamSetu simplifies the process through an intuitive and mobile-friendly experience.

Our goal is to create a platform that is accessible, transparent, and easy to use for everyone — even users with limited technical experience.

Recruiters can quickly connect with verified workers, while job seekers can discover nearby opportunities that match their skills, preferences, and salary expectations.
